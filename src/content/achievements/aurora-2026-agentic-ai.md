## Result

**Team Knurdz Neural** was **selected as a finalist** in the undergraduate **Agentic AI Challenge** at **[Aurora 2026](https://aurora-2026.vercel.app/)** — Sri Lanka's premier university computing competition, organized by the **Computer Science Association** in collaboration with the **Department of Computer Science, University of Sri Jayewardenepura**.

Knurdz Neural advanced through the proposal review stage and was named among the teams selected to continue in the 2026 competition programme.

## About the competition

**Aurora** is the annual Conference on Computer Science, spanning keynote sessions, workshops, and a competitive programme for undergraduates and school students. **Aurora 2026** centres on **Agentic AI and Social Engineering** — how autonomous systems can detect, explain, and respond to social-engineering threats such as phishing, urgency manipulation, and authority abuse.

The undergraduate track asks teams of four to design agentic systems with clear architecture, ethical safeguards, and measurable impact — evaluated on innovation, technical feasibility, ethical compliance, and presentation quality.
