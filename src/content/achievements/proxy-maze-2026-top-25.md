## Result

**Team Knurdz** (University of Moratuwa) was named among the **top 25 teams** in **[Proxy Maze '26](https://proxymaze.torchproxies.com/)** after **Challenge 1** — the 24-hour technical sprint that opened the build journey for this edition.

Knurdz advanced past the first filter alongside teams from universities across Sri Lanka. The hackathon remains **in progress**, with further stages ahead in the 14-week programme.

[Proxy Maze '26 →](https://proxymaze.torchproxies.com/)

## About the competition

**Proxy Maze '26** is a **14-week university hackathon** presented by **Torch Proxies** (Torch Labs), focused on building original solutions that use proxy infrastructure as a core part of the stack.

Teams of **2–4 students** from the same university move through registration, technical challenges, proposal review, guided build phases, and mentorship workshops. Challenge 1 tested core skills and practical use of Torch Proxies products; the programme continues with proposal and implementation stages for the teams that qualified.
