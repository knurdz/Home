## Result

**Team Knurdz** was **selected** for the **University Category** of **[IESL RoboGames 25/26](https://robo.cse.mrt.ac.lk/university)**: the undergraduate robotics programme organized by the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Moratuwa**, in association with the **Institute of Engineers, Sri Lanka (IESL)**.

Knurdz appears on the official shortlist alongside FalconX, NET, Net Runners, Segfault, Team Zeus, and Tech Titans.

[University category →](https://robo.cse.mrt.ac.lk/university)

## About the competition

RoboGames University Category is an **undergraduate challenge** in advanced robotics programming, run through **Webots simulation** and progressive technical rounds designed to test algorithm design, consistency, and engineering discipline.

The programme is one of Sri Lanka's established university-level robotics competitions, bringing together teams from institutions across the country under the RoboGames banner.
