## Result

**Team Knurdz** was named **winner** of the **mini competition** at **[IDEALIZE 2026](https://idealize.aiesec.lk/)**, organized by **AIESEC in University of Moratuwa**.

Knurdz topped the **Agentic AI & MCP** mini challenge, building **intelligent, tool-connected AI systems**, while the broader IDEALIZE programme runs as an **island-wide** mobile and web development competition for Sri Lankan youth.

[IDEALIZE 2026 →](https://idealize.aiesec.lk/)

## About the event

**IDEALIZE** is an **all-island mobile app and web development competition** run by **AIESEC in University of Moratuwa**, giving participants mentorship, workshops, and a path from idea to working product. Editions welcome undergraduates and young builders from across Sri Lanka, with judging that rewards innovation, feasibility, and impact.

**IDEALIZE 2026** adds a dedicated **mini competition** focused on **Agentic AI** and **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** solutions: a side track where teams compete on the frontier of agent-based software. The main programme continues with proposal, build, and go-to-market stages; the mini competition opens and closes on its own timeline before teams move into later phases.

AIESEC’s global emphasis on **leadership, collaboration, and purpose-driven innovation** shapes IDEALIZE as both a technical contest and a launchpad for the next generation of Sri Lankan product builders.
