## Result

**Two teams from Knurdz** (University of Moratuwa) **reached the finale** at **[RoboRoarZ International](https://www.roboroarz.com/)** in the **tertiary league**, competing on **Smorphi**, the reconfigurable robot platform developed at the **Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD)**.

Both Knurdz squads advanced through training, design, and competition rounds alongside institutions from **multiple countries**, including teams from **Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines, India, Malaysia, Oman**, and beyond.

[RoboRoarZ →](https://www.roboroarz.com/)

## About the competition

**RoboRoarZ** is an international, **design-centred reconfigurable robotics** programme and competition that promotes **STEAM** education from primary through tertiary levels. Teams **program and configure Smorphi robots** to meet a yearly theme, with challenges that span **autonomous physical missions**, **piloted tasks**, and **simulation** rounds.

The tertiary league brings together polytechnic and university teams for multi-day events, briefings, robot training, development, qualifying rounds, and **finals**, with judging focused on strategy, completion time, and innovation in problem-solving. **RoboRoarZ International** editions draw hundreds of students from schools and higher-education institutions across the region, anchored by partners including **SUTD**, **Wefaa Robotics**, and the wider IEEE robotics community.
