## Event

**[Cursor 24H Buildathon 2026 · Colombo](https://buildathon.cursorsrilanka.com/)** was Sri Lanka's largest 24-hour buildathon, organized by [TechTalk360](https://buildathon.cursorsrilanka.com/) and held at **Royal-MAS Arena, Colombo** on **May 16–17, 2026**.

Builders had a single ship deadline: registration from 7:00 AM on May 16, the build clock starting at 10:00 AM, submissions due at 10:00 AM on May 17, and final presentations from 12:15 PM. The event brought together hundreds of in-person builders working in teams, with partner stacks spanning voice AI, multimodal models, backend, automation, deploy, and fintech.

## Our result

**Team Knurdz 3.0** was named the winner of the **[n8n track](https://buildathon.cursorsrilanka.com/winners)** — *Best use of n8n* — for our project **Nexus OS**.

The n8n track focused on fair-code workflow automation: connecting services, orchestrating agents, and shipping automations that hold up under real demo pressure. Nexus OS was built to showcase deep use of that stack within the 24-hour window.

Official results: [Winners · Cursor Buildathon Colombo](https://buildathon.cursorsrilanka.com/winners)

## How winners were chosen

1. **Global scoring** — 30 judges worldwide reviewed every project on craft, ambition, partner-stack use, and demo quality.
2. **Track shortlist** — The top-scoring team in each of the 10 partner tracks earned a live finals slot in Colombo.
3. **Live finals** — Finalists pitched on stage to the Sri Lankan judge pool: **5 minutes to present**, **3 minutes of Q&A**.

## Partner tracks at the buildathon

The competition ran across 10 partner tracks, including ElevenLabs, Gemini, Beyond Presence, Convex, **n8n**, Netlify, Fal, MiniMax, VALSEA, and Seylan — each team committed to exactly one track for the event.
