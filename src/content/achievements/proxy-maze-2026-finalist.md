## Result

**Team Knurdz** (University of Moratuwa) competed in **[Proxy Maze '26](https://proxymaze.torchproxies.com/)**, Torch Proxies' **14-week university hackathon** for proxy-based innovation.

Knurdz was named among the **top 25 teams** after **Challenge 1**, advanced to the **top 8**, and **reached the finale**. The team also received the **Most Popular Team** award.

[Proxy Maze '26 →](https://proxymaze.torchproxies.com/)

## About the competition

**Proxy Maze '26** is a **14-week university hackathon** presented by **Torch Proxies** (Torch Labs), focused on building original solutions that use proxy infrastructure as a core part of the stack.

Teams of **2–4 students** from the same university move through registration, technical challenges, proposal review, guided build phases, and mentorship workshops. Challenge 1 tested core skills and practical use of Torch Proxies products; later stages narrowed the field through proposal review and implementation rounds to the finale.
