## Result

**Knurdz** swept the **top three places** in **Logic Sprint** at **IEEE Open Week 2026**, hosted by the **[IEEE Student Branch, University of Moratuwa](https://ieeesb.uom.lk/)**.

After four days of daily challenges, the final leaderboard placed **Knurdz members** in **1st**, **2nd**, and **3rd**:

2. **Sadeepaa**
3. **Kasun**

[IEEE Student Branch · UoM →](https://ieeesb.uom.lk/)

## About the competition

**IEEE Open Week** is the annual showcase of the University of Moratuwa IEEE Student Branch and its chapters: stalls, sessions, and student-run competitions that introduce undergraduates to IEEE societies and technical communities on campus.

**Logic Sprint** ran across **four days** as a logic-focused challenge under the Open Week programme, with cumulative scoring and a final top-five leaderboard published after Day 04.
