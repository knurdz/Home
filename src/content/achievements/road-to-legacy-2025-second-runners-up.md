## Result

**Team Knurdz** was named **2nd runners-up** at **[Road to Legacy 2.0](https://www.roadtolegacy.team/)** and **won the mini competition** held during the programme’s **inter-university meetup**, bringing together students from the **University of Moratuwa (UOM)**, **University of Colombo (UOC)**, and **University of Sri Jayewardenepura (USJ)**.

Knurdz competed alongside delegates from all three partner universities, combining teamwork across campuses with the skills tested in the on-day mini challenge.

[Road to Legacy →](https://www.roadtolegacy.team/)

## About the event

**Road to Legacy** is a student-led initiative that **bridges academia and industry** for **first-year IT undergraduates** in Sri Lanka. Organized through collaboration between students at **UOM**, **UOC**, and **USJ** (including the **IEEE Student Branch at USJ**), the programme runs **cross-university meetups**, networking, and a flagship conference-style event.

**Road to Legacy 2.0** (August 2025) united **550+ delegates** at the **Faculty of Technology, USJ**, with a large organizing committee drawn from all three universities. Meetups beforehand focused on **connection and collaboration** across campuses, games, networking, and team challenges, before the main event’s sessions and competitions.
