## Result

**Team Knurdz** competed at the **Xeynergy Mini Competition**, a compact build event hosted by **[Xeynergy](https://www.xeynergy.com/)**: a Colombo-based technology company delivering software and digital solutions.

Knurdz joined other teams in a time-boxed challenge centred on **shipping working software quickly**, using modern tooling and **AI-assisted development** under real deadline pressure.

[Xeynergy →](https://www.xeynergy.com/)

## About the event

**Xeynergy** runs culture and community programmes that encourage **innovation, collaboration, and hands-on engineering**: from internal hackathons to guest-judged competitions. Mini competitions and hackathon-style events (including initiatives such as **VibeCodeIt**) give teams a short window to move from idea to demo, with judging that weighs **execution speed, solution quality, and teamwork**.

These events connect Sri Lanka’s builder community with industry practitioners and are designed to mirror the pace and creativity expected in professional product teams.
