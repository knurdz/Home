## Result

**Knurdz** placed **third worldwide** in **[Zero to Agent](https://community.vercel.com/hackathons/zero-to-agent)** with **Origami**, a global build week hosted by **Vercel** and **v0** for shipping production AI agents.

**Origami** was the **highest-ranked submission from Sri Lanka** in the competition. First and second place went to teams based outside Sri Lanka, placing Knurdz on the global podium as the leading project from the Sri Lankan builder community in this edition.

[View official winners →](https://community.vercel.com/hackathons/zero-to-agent/winners)

## About the competition

Zero to Agent ran **April 24 – May 4, 2026**, bringing together hundreds of builders across **388 submissions**. Projects were evaluated through community voting and expert review, with winners deploying real agents on **v0** and **Vercel**.

## Origami

Origami turns GitHub repositories, PDFs, and raw text into a live interactive canvas and a v0-powered MVP — reducing the gap between understanding complex source material and shipping a working prototype.

Built and deployed on the Vercel stack, the project was recognized for strong use of **v0**, clear product narrative, and a polished live demo.
